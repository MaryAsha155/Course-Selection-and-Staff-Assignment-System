About Us

College:RGMCET


Welcome to RGMCET, a beacon of academic excellence and innovation since 1996. Located in Nandyal, our institution is dedicated to nurturing the next generation of leaders, thinkers, and innovators. At RGMCET, we strive to provide a transformative educational experience that empowers students to reach their full potential. We are committed to fostering critical thinking, creativity, and a lifelong passion for learning. We RGMCET vision for knowledge, prosperity and perseverance for aspiring individuals.

We uphold core values of academic excellence, innovation, inclusivity, integrity, and community engagement. These principles guide our approach to education and shape our campus culture. Our distinguished faculty comprises leading experts in their fields, dedicated to both teaching and research.
